    Platform rules and running costs

    What WhatsApp charges, and what it allows

    Replies to customersFree within 24 h

    Replies inside the 24-hour window after a customer writes are free under Meta's current pricing page. Some providers report charges from 1 Oct 2026, so we're confirming that.

    Order updates laterLow per message

    "Utility" templates are used for order updates sent after the window closes. Indicative US rate is about $0.004 each. Rates vary by country.

    PromotionsHighest cost

    Marketing templates, about $0.025 each in the US. They're only sent to customers who opted in, and each campaign gets spend approval.

    AI policyWe're allowed

    Since 15 Jan 2026 Meta bans general-purpose AI chatbots, but business bots that take orders are still permitted. Ours stays scoped to the shop's products.

    Sources: Meta pricing docs, Hyperleap US calculator, respond.io on the AI policy. The full research note is in our internal repo.

    Next steps

    What gets us to the first live shop

    1. Pick a pilot shop and market. Their catalogue replaces the sample, and their country's rates set our cost per order.
    2. Set up the Meta business account. Separate test and live apps, plus a secure public address for the workflow.
    3. Connect and test the AI model. Measure how often it reads orders correctly before any customer sees it.
    4. Turn on outgoing messages. Approved templates, retries, and opt-in records. The team signs off on the first live message.
    Kochi Kitchenware, the customer, the products and the prices are examples for this walkthrough. No real customer data is used, and the demo sends no WhatsApp messages.
